💡 Training Protocol

    

      
Step 1: Go to Training Ground

      
Step 2: Pick M416 with Compensator + Vertical Grip

      
Step 3: Spray at wall from 30m — observe pattern

      
Step 4: Tilt phone DOWN slightly while spraying (gyro recoil control)

      
Step 5: If spray goes UP → increase gyro by 10-20%

      
Step 6: If spray goes DOWN → decrease gyro by 10-20%

      
Step 7: Repeat at 50m, 100m, 200m distances

      
Step 8: Switch to AKM — repeat Steps 3-7

      
Goal: Flat spray pattern = near-zero visible recoil
