# Plus Two Chemistry — SAY Exam 2026 (Kerala DHSE) · Crash Study App

A single-file, offline-capable revision app for the **Kerala Higher Secondary
Plus Two (Class 12) Chemistry SAY exam 2026**. Open `index.html` in any browser —
no install, no internet needed after the first load.

## What's inside

- **All 16 chapters** of the Kerala/SCERT Class 12 Chemistry syllabus, each with:
  - Important topics explained in plain language (collapsible).
  - Must-know **formulas and named reactions**.
  - A **"How questions come from this chapter"** box (1 / 2 / 3 / 4-mark framing).
  - **Practice questions with full model answers** written the way the examiner wants.
- **Weightage tags** (High / Medium / Core) so you study the big scorers first.
- An **8-hour study plan** and a "if you only have time for the big scorers" list.
- **Quiz me** mode — shuffles every practice question for self-testing.
- **Countdown timer** to your exam (tap "set" to enter your own hours).
- **Search**, **light/dark theme**, **print to PDF**, and chapter "done" ticking
  (saved in your browser).
- The **exam pattern** (60-mark theory, 2 hrs + 15 min cool-off, choice structure)
  and an **answer-writing tips** section for grabbing easy marks.

## Built from

Official syllabus and exam-pattern references (Kerala DHSE), linked in the app
footer. All explanations and questions are original revision material based on the
standard Kerala/NCERT Class 12 Chemistry syllabus. **Verify the live mark scheme on
[dhsekerala.gov.in](https://www.dhsekerala.gov.in) on exam day** — boards can revise
the pattern.

## Honest note

No app can predict the exact paper. These are high-probability practice questions
built from the real syllabus and past-paper patterns, with model answers and
mark-scoring tips. Use them to revise actively (write the answers, don't just read).
